Club Etiquette & Care Rules

Club Etiquette ✋

A good night of badminton is easy — respect the court, use the peg board correctly and look after your shuttles.

  • 👟 Non-Marking Court Shoes

    Sole must not mark or scuff our non-marking floors — no rubber-soled runs shoes, boots or trainers. Clean white/low-profile soles preferred.

  • 📌 Peg-Board Rotation

    When a court is taken, everyone moves up by one peg. Ranked ladders and booking rotation are published on the club WhatsApp group — members only.

  • 🪶 Shuttle Care

    Collect spent shuttles after each set and keep them dry out of the sun. Feather packs last much longer when stored in a case.

  • 🤝 Respect & Fair Play

    Play to the rules, shake hands at every game and accept line calls gracefully — kindness keeps everyone on the court.
